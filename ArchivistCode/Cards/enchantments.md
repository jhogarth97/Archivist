## Proposed Enchantments

- Sharp	- This card deals 3 additional damage.
- Swift	- This card draws 1 card when played.


- Scribed - This card deals 2 additional damage and applies 1 Vulnerable.
- Edge - This card deals 3 additional damage to enemies with a Feather stuck to them.
- Crescendo - This card deals 1 additional damage for each card you have played this turn.
- Bloodquill - This card deals 4 additional damage. Lose 2 HP when you play it.


- Bindery - This card gains 3 additional Block.
- Vellum - When you play this card, gain 1 Block for each card in your hand.
- Blotting - This card gains Retain. When you play it, draw 1 card.


- Gloss - This card costs 1 less this combat.
- Marginal - This card costs 1 less, and Exhausts when played.
- Overleaf - When you play this card, draw 1 card, then discard 1 card.
- Catalogue	- When you draw this card, reduce its cost by 1 this combat.
- Erratum - This card's effects trigger an additional time, then it gains Exhaust.
- Recursive - The first time you play this card each turn, add a copy to your Discard Pile.
- Thrift - This card costs 0 this combat, but you gain 1 less energy next turn.


- Lucid - When you play this card, gain 2 Waking.
- Reverent - When you play this card, your Waking triggers twice at the start of your next turn.


- Downy - This card sticks 1 additional Feather. If at cap, deal 4 damage instead.
- Moult - When you play this card, return 1 stuck Feather and gain 3 Block.
